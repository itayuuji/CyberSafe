📚 Learning Modules

Seven essential topics to help you recognize and avoid cyber threats.

🎣 Phishing

Phishing is when attackers send fake emails, SMS, or messages pretending to be a trusted organization (bank, government, company) to steal your personal or financial information.

⚠️ DangerMessages urging "immediate action" or threatening account suspension are classic phishing tactics.
💡 TipAlways check the sender's email address or phone number carefully — small spelling changes are a red flag.
✅ Safe PracticeNever click links in unexpected messages. Visit the official website directly by typing the URL yourself.

💸 UPI & Financial Fraud

Fraudsters trick users into scanning QR codes or sharing UPI PINs, tricking them into "receiving" money when they are actually sending it.

⚠️ DangerYou never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money — only to send it.
💡 TipBe cautious of strangers asking you to scan a QR code to claim a prize or refund.
✅ Safe PracticeVerify the payee name before confirming any UPI transaction, and never share your PIN or OTP with anyone.

🔐 Password Security & 2FA

Weak or reused passwords are one of the easiest ways for accounts to be hacked. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds an extra layer of protection.

⚠️ DangerUsing the same password across multiple accounts means one leak can compromise everything.
💡 TipUse a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols — at least 12 characters long.
✅ Safe PracticeEnable 2FA wherever possible, especially on email, banking, and social media accounts.

📱 Social Media Privacy

Oversharing personal details on social media can expose you to stalking, identity theft, and targeted scams.

⚠️ DangerPublic posts with your location, phone number, or daily routine can be misused by strangers.
💡 TipRegularly review your privacy settings and limit who can see your posts and personal info.
✅ Safe PracticeAvoid accepting friend/follow requests from unknown accounts and think twice before sharing location tags.

😢 Cyberbullying

Cyberbullying includes harassment, threats, or humiliation carried out through digital platforms, and can have serious emotional effects.

⚠️ DangerIgnoring cyberbullying can allow it to escalate — it is not something to "just deal with."
💡 TipTake screenshots as evidence before blocking or reporting the bully.
✅ Safe PracticeReport abusive behaviour to the platform and talk to a trusted adult, teacher, or counsellor.

📰 Fake News

Misinformation spreads quickly on social media and messaging apps, often designed to mislead, provoke fear, or manipulate opinions.

⚠️ DangerForwarding unverified news, especially on WhatsApp, can contribute to panic or even legal consequences.
💡 TipCheck multiple credible news sources before believing or sharing a claim.
✅ Safe PracticeUse fact-checking sites and look for the original source before forwarding any message.

🚨 Cybercrime Reporting

India has a dedicated system for reporting cybercrimes quickly, which is crucial for recovering lost funds and catching offenders.

⚠️ DangerDelaying a report reduces the chances of recovering money lost in financial fraud.
💡 TipReport within the "Golden Hour" — the first hour after the incident — for the best chance of fund recovery.
✅ Safe PracticeCall the National Cybercrime Helpline 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in immediately.
