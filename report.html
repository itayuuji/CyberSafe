📞 Cybercrime Reporting

If you've faced online fraud, act fast. Here's exactly what to do.

National Cybercrime Helpline
📞 1930
Call immediately if you've lost money to online fraud

Also report online at cybercrime.gov.in — the Government of India's official portal for cybercrime complaints.

⏱️ The Golden Hour The first hour after a financial fraud is called the "Golden Hour." Reporting within this window on the 1930 helpline dramatically increases the chance banks can freeze/reverse the transaction before the money is withdrawn.

What To Do After Fraud

  1. 1 Immediately call the National Cybercrime Helpline 1930 — don't wait, every minute matters.
  2. 2 File a detailed complaint at cybercrime.gov.in with transaction IDs, screenshots, and timestamps.
  3. 3 Contact your bank's fraud helpline to block your card/account and request a transaction freeze.
  4. 4 Change passwords and enable 2FA on any accounts that may have been compromised.
  5. 5 Save all evidence — SMS, emails, screenshots, call recordings — for the investigation.
  6. 6 Visit your nearest cyber police station if online reporting isn't possible, and get an acknowledgement/FIR copy.
  7. 7 Monitor your accounts and credit report for a few weeks for any further suspicious activity.
